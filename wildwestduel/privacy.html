WILD WEST DUEL — PRIVACY POLICY
Last updated: 2 November 2026

This is an English translation of the Turkish privacy policy (https://gdgamestudio.com/wildwestduel/gizlilik).
If the two differ, the Turkish text prevails.

This policy explains which data the Wild West Duel game ("the Game") collects and why, where and for how long it
is kept, and your rights. Operator of the Game (data controller): GD-GAMES. Contact: destek@gdgamestudio.com.

1. DATA WE COLLECT

a) Player name: the name you type in the main menu. It is shown to your opponent, on the leaderboard and in match
   history. You do not need to use your real name.
b) Guest account: when the Game first connects to the server, the server creates a random account ID and a
   secret key. They are stored on your device. The server keeps only a one-way hash of the key, never the key
   itself. No e-mail address, phone number, password or real identity is asked for. You can see your account ID
   and key under PROFİL > HESAP and use them to sign in to the same account on another device; do not share the
   key with anyone.
c) Game progress: level, experience points, duel rating, numbers of wins / losses / draws, best reaction time,
   unlocked cosmetics and your character's appearance.
d) Match records: time of the match, the opponent's player name, the result, reaction times, rating and
   experience changes.
e) Connection data: your IP address and network latency while you are connected. The IP address is used only in
   memory, to make the connection and to limit abuse (e.g. the number of accounts created from one address); it is
   not written to match records or to the account. Connection IPs may appear in the server's operation logs.
f) Error reports: if the Game catches an error or closes unexpectedly, it sends a short report to the server: the
   error text, the lines of code where it happened, the game version, the operating system version and the device
   model. Reports do not contain your name, account ID or location.
g) Stored on the device: settings (player name), your chosen character and outfit, the account ID and key, and the
   last known profile summary. These stay on your device only.
h) Purchases: if you buy the Gold Season Pass through Google Play, the server verifies the purchase receipt issued
   by Google Play (purchase token, product, order time and Google's signature) and stores the purchase token, the
   product, the season and the time together with your account. We never see your card, bank or billing details;
   Google Play takes the payment.
i) Gang data: the name and tag of a gang you found, your memberships and weekly gang points. Gang names and tags
   are shown to other players.

The Game does not access your location, contacts, camera, microphone, advertising ID or data in other apps. It
shows no ads and uses no third-party analytics or advertising services.

Web site (gdgamestudio.com): it uses no cookies, analytics or tracking code and asks you for no data. The site is
hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.); GitHub may keep visitors' IP addresses in technical access logs for security
and operation. GitHub's privacy statement:
https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement

Third-party services (for purchases only):
- Google Play Billing (Google): takes and processes the payment. Google's privacy policy:
  https://policies.google.com/privacy
- Unity In-App Purchasing (Unity Technologies): the software library through which the Game talks to Google Play.
  According to Unity, this library collects purchase history, device and user identifiers, approximate location,
  app diagnostics and crash logs for purchase functionality and analytics; it sends this data to Unity over an
  encrypted connection and does not share it with others. Unity's privacy policy:
  https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy

2. PURPOSES

We use the data only to: run online duels and referee their results, match players of similar skill, keep your
progress, the leaderboard and match history, verify purchases and credit them to your account and prevent the
same purchase from being used twice, prevent cheating and abuse, and find and fix errors. Data is not sold,
rented or used for advertising.

3. WHERE AND HOW LONG DATA IS KEPT

Data is stored on a server owned by the operator of the Game (Turkey).
- Account and progress: until you delete your account.
- Match records: they stay in your opponents' match history after your account is deleted, but your name is
  replaced with "Silinmiş oyuncu" (deleted player) and the link to the account is removed.
- Error reports: 90 days, then deleted automatically.
- Purchase records: kept after the account is deleted, to prevent the same purchase from being used twice and
  for refunds or legal obligations.
- Gang data: when the account is deleted, your gang membership is deleted; a gang of which you are the only
  member is closed.

Security: the connection between the Game and the server is encrypted with TLS (wss:// / https://,
api.gdgamestudio.com). Versions of the Game released before 2 November 2026 use an unencrypted connection; the
unencrypted connection kept open for them will be closed once players have updated. We recommend keeping the Game
up to date. The Game never asks for a password, e-mail address or payment details; payment happens in Google
Play's own encrypted screen.

4. YOUR RIGHTS (TURKISH LAW NO. 6698 ON THE PROTECTION OF PERSONAL DATA AND SIMILAR LAWS)

You have the right to learn whether your data is processed, to request information, to request correction or
deletion, and to object.
- You can delete your account in the Game: PROFİL > HESABIMI SİL. Deletion happens on the server immediately and
  cannot be undone.
- Uninstalling the app deletes the data on the device, but not the account on the server (delete it from PROFİL
  first).
- If you cannot open the app or have uninstalled it, you can request deletion by e-mail to
  destek@gdgamestudio.com, stating your account ID (PROFİL > HESAP). Details:
  https://gdgamestudio.com/wildwestduel/hesap-silme
- Other requests: destek@gdgamestudio.com. Requests are answered within 30 days.

5. CHILDREN

The Game is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them. The Game contains
cartoon-style gun and duel scenes.

6. CHANGES

If this policy changes, the current text is published in the Game and at
https://gdgamestudio.com/wildwestduel/gizlilik, and the date is updated.
