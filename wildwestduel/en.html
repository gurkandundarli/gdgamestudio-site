A Wild West reflex duel. Draw fast; the first to fire wins.

The game

Two gunslingers face each other. After "HAZIR OL!" (get ready!), a letter or a swipe command appears on the screen: whoever draws it correctly before the opponent fires. Jumping the gun is punished, and red decoy commands must be ignored. The server decides the result and measures reaction times to the millisecond. The game is in Turkish.

  • Ranked online duels, leagues and a trophy road
  • Two players on one device, private rooms with a friend and 2v2 team duels
  • Bandit campaign, tournaments, bounty hunts and ghost opponents
  • Daily quests, achievements, gangs and season rewards
  • Choose your character, outfit colour, weapon skin and victory pose
